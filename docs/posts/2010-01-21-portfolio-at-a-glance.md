## CMS

Have implemented, configured and worked with DNN, Umbraco, Drupal, Joomla, Modx, SilverStripe
 
Also keep an eye on Sitefinity, SiteCore, Kooboo, N2, MojoPortal
 
ASP.Net DNN - Mantra DotNeNuke - skinning, customization

**MyBrainSolutions** - Required a custom solution to provide flash based training for users interested in improving their brain health. I designed the website architecture with a mix of ASP.Net 3.5 technologies such as linq to sql, linq to xml, json, flash, xml web services that also interfaced with their online external database (java based) via REST.
 
**MSN FriendMagnet** - Complex flash based promotional game for all MSN messenger users in australia. This was developed with ASP.Net 3.5 using Flash, Json, Linq, NHibernate, Custom web handlers, Web Farms. I was one of several developers working on various aspects of the application.
 
**Emstream** - Developed a multi-channel music system from the ground up, http://www.emstream.com.au/splash.php. Working very closely with the IT Manager, and Emstream, I designed and developed the Music System Software to cater to multiple channels within venues such as Ivy Bar, Establishment Hotel in Sydney and across Australia. Developed using a wide variety of technologies including VB6, C# .Net, COM, 3rd Party tools and scripts.

**Pedigree Puppy.com** - online portal for puppies, users can select puppies based on their lifestyles, track puppies progress as they grow, helpful hints and tips to train and grow their puppies. I designed the ASP.Net 2.0 website architecture, wrote functional specifications documentation, xml web services, researched and implemented video formats and streaming.
 
**Berlei** - wrote functional specifications docs and researched and recommended website architecture for custom storefront to advertise all their products.

**Prokennex Australia** - Had a static HTML table based site, I rebuilt their design to a tableless css based design and implemented modX CMS, customised with an image gallery
 
**Discover Performance** - required online payment processing for their Modx based site. I developed the processor to interface their banks payment gateway with modx, to create member subscriptions with specific groups. http://www.discoverperformance.com/

**Fairfax industries** had developed a custom CMS application in ASP.Net for their community newspapers over 7 months which was about to be cancelled due to excessive costs and failed deadlines, they required a solution fast . I recommended and helped implement Umbraco CMS with version control to handle large amounts of content across their community newspapers.
 
**Nuplex industries** requested a system that could handle multiple sites in multiple languages across the globe, with enterprise level features such as version control for content, different levels of user access, along with a career board for posting job vacancies within Nuplex globally and allowing applicants to apply online with resume. I implemented Drupal CMS, customised and built modules, Nuplex currently ran Drupal for their Intranet systems.
 
**Xforce Automotive** required a CMS to handle their car and accessories inventory, with lightbox images showing their automotive equipment available for different cars across brands. I built a CMS using CakePHP framework and fckEditor richtext editor.
 
**Enterprise Initiatives** legal required a clean simple CMS to provide content about their company info with a WYSIWYG editor. I developed a custom CMS to handle managed content for their pages.
 
**The Wedding Show** - Required an application to provide an online TV experience related to weddings, with multiple channels. I analyzed the client requirements through meetings, wrote technical documentation, researched video streaming, and recommended overall technology decisions
 
**Netcomm** -  Maintenance and custom development for administration backend.
